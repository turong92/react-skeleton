This explains how cancelling and refunds work for {{serviceName}} subscriptions provided by {{companyName}} ("we"). Payment details are handled as described in the [privacy policy](/privacy).

# 1. How to cancel

In "My subscription" you can pick one of two buttons.

- **End at period end**: you keep using the service until the period you paid for ends, and you are not charged again. There is no refund.
- **End now and refund**: the subscription ends right away, and we refund the part you have not used, as set out below.

"End now and refund" is available only for subscriptions paid in South Korea. The country you choose when you subscribe decides this. For other countries only "End at period end" is available.

# 2. Withdraw within 7 days for a full refund

If you end the subscription within 7 days of the start of the current billing period and you have not used the service, we refund everything you paid. There is no fee.

# 3. After 7 days, or if you have used it

The refund is calculated like this:

> amount paid − the part you used − the fee

- **The part you used**: for a monthly subscription it is the elapsed share of the period. For a subscription longer than a month (for example yearly) it is the number of months already begun times the monthly price of the same tier; a yearly discount does not count toward the part you used.
- **The fee**: 10% of what is left after the part you used. Fractions of the smallest currency unit are dropped.

The screen shows the refund and how it is calculated before you press anything. The amount shrinks as the period runs down, and it is calculated at the moment you press the button.

# 4. When the money arrives

We refund to the card you paid with. Depending on your card company it can take a few days to show up.

# 5. Foreign cards

We do not notify you about amounts that differ because of exchange rates, and we are not responsible for such differences.
